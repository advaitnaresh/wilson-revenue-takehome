The final QC stage found at least one hard failure (see the comparison above, or the Stage 6 card higher up) — the same condition that makes pipeline.run() raise and exit non-zero in the Python/Spark versions instead of writing output files. Revenue numbers computed from a table that failed validation aren't trustworthy, so this page withholds them rather than show them anyway. The health, comparison, and change summary above are unaffected — they describe what happened, not the untrusted result.
